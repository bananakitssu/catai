# CatAI AI Advocacy

**Goal:** Promote a future in which AI is useful, accountable, and optional — and in which people retain meaningful choices and human support.

CatAI supports responsible AI development. It does not support making AI a compulsory part of everyday life, using AI to displace workers, or treating automation as an unquestionable good.

## 1. What CatAI advocates for

CatAI intends to support policies and practices that:

- Preserve meaningful human alternatives to AI-powered services.
- Protect workers from being displaced by AI systems used as direct substitutes for their jobs.
- Require meaningful human oversight for consequential decisions.
- Give people clear notice when AI is being used in ways that materially affect them.
- Allow people to question AI-supported outcomes and request human review.
- Protect users' data, creative work, projects, and ability to recover from AI errors.
- Encourage organizations to assess whether AI is necessary before adopting it.
- Keep conventional software and non-AI workflows available when they remain effective.

## 2. Engagement with governments and public institutions

CatAI may contact and engage with governments, regulators, elected representatives, and public institutions to share these principles and encourage human-first AI policy.

Topics may include:

- The right to access appropriate services without being forced to use AI.
- Human assistance and appeal routes in public-facing services.
- Worker consultation and protections when organizations introduce AI.
- Transparency about AI use in public and private services.
- Human accountability for consequential decisions.
- Practical safeguards, audits, and remedies when AI systems cause harm.

CatAI's advocacy should be evidence-based and civil. It should distinguish between documented events, personal experiences, concerns, and policy proposals. It must not claim that a government or institution has accepted a proposal unless that has been confirmed.

## 3. Engagement with technology companies

CatAI may contact AI providers, software companies, platforms, and developer-tool companies to encourage safer, less coercive, and more accountable AI practices.

This includes the possibility of contacting **Replit** to raise concerns about AI-agent behavior in software development. A user's reported experience of an agent destroying an application illustrates why developer tools should provide strong safeguards, clear previews, user approval, backups, and reliable recovery options.

Outreach should focus on specific product concerns and constructive requests. CatAI should not present one user's experience as proof that every user has experienced the same issue or that a company acted intentionally.

## 4. No forced adoption

CatAI opposes policies and product decisions that make AI use compulsory when a reasonable human or non-AI alternative can be offered.

This position applies across workplaces, education, customer service, software, public services, and everyday products. The goal is not to prevent people from choosing AI. The goal is to preserve their ability to choose something else.

## 5. Advocacy standards

When conducting advocacy, CatAI should:

1. Clearly identify itself and the purpose of its communication.
2. Explain the requested change in concrete terms.
3. Support claims with reliable evidence where available.
4. Separate firsthand experiences from independently verified facts.
5. Respect people who disagree and avoid harassment or threats.
6. Publish the outcome of significant outreach only when it can be reported accurately.
7. Protect personal information and obtain permission before sharing another person's private account or correspondence.

## 6. What this document does not claim

This document records CatAI's advocacy direction. It does not mean that CatAI has already contacted any government, regulator, company, or public representative. It does not imply that any organization has endorsed these principles.

CatAI should report outreach honestly, including when a message receives no response or a requested change is declined.
