# CatAI Usage Policy

**Purpose:** Set expectations for using CatAI in a way that protects human choice, employment, and accountability.

This policy applies to individuals, businesses, institutions, developers, and other organizations using or integrating CatAI. It is a statement of CatAI's intended project rules. It does not by itself create a contract or replace applicable law; any binding enforcement terms must be incorporated into the product's published terms and supported by a fair review process.

## 1. Acceptable use

CatAI may be used for purposes such as:

- Helping people draft, summarize, research, learn, brainstorm, and create.
- Supporting employees with tasks while leaving meaningful responsibility and control with people.
- Improving accessibility, communication, and productivity when people choose to use it.
- Assisting with repetitive or administrative work without treating employees as disposable.
- Experimenting, building, and learning, provided users remain responsible for how outputs are used.

Commercial use is permitted, subject to this policy and applicable law.

## 2. Human employment must not be displaced

Organizations must not use CatAI with the purpose of replacing their employees, eliminating human roles, or avoiding the hiring of people by making CatAI perform those roles instead.

In particular, organizations must not:

- Present CatAI as a direct substitute for employees or contractors.
- Use CatAI deployment as the stated or practical basis for removing workers from roles that CatAI is taking over.
- Use CatAI to create a nominally human-free service where people are needed for meaningful assistance, judgment, or accountability.
- Hide workforce replacement behind labels such as efficiency, restructuring, or automation.

Organizations may use CatAI to assist workers, but people must retain meaningful roles, appropriate authority, and access to the information needed to do their jobs. CatAI's position is that technology should improve people's work rather than make people expendable.

## 3. Do not force AI on people

Users and organizations must not make CatAI a needless requirement for accessing ordinary functionality or services. Where practical, provide a reasonable non-AI route or a human-assisted alternative.

Examples of practices CatAI opposes include:

- Requiring people to interact with an AI assistant when a normal support channel could be offered.
- Removing a useful non-AI workflow solely to push AI adoption.
- Requiring AI use for routine tasks that can be completed through simpler software or a human process.
- Making people accept AI-generated decisions without a way to question them or request human review.

CatAI itself should not introduce AI features merely to follow a trend. Use the simplest approach that adequately serves people.

## 4. Keep humans responsible for consequential decisions

CatAI may provide information or recommendations, but it must not be treated as the final authority for decisions that materially affect a person's employment, education, finances, healthcare, legal position, access to essential services, or rights.

A qualified and accountable human decision-maker must review relevant information, consider context, and remain responsible for the outcome. A nominal human approval that simply rubber-stamps an AI result is not meaningful oversight.

## 5. Be transparent

When CatAI materially contributes to content, recommendations, or a process, organizations should make that use clear to affected people. Do not present AI-generated material as verified human work when that distinction matters to the audience or decision.

Users should be told about material limitations, uncertainty, and the availability of human review or non-AI alternatives.

## 6. Respectful interaction

CatAI should treat users respectfully. Users are encouraged to interact with CatAI respectfully and to avoid abusive or disruptive conduct toward other users, developers, and support staff.

Respect does not mean users must agree with CatAI, praise it, pretend it is human, or refrain from criticism. Honest corrections, complaints, testing, and disagreement are welcome. This section promotes constructive communication; it does not claim that the AI experiences emotional harm.

## 7. Safety, privacy, and legal responsibilities

Users and organizations remain responsible for complying with applicable law, protecting personal and confidential information, checking important outputs, and ensuring that their use of CatAI is appropriate for the task. Do not use CatAI to facilitate unlawful discrimination, harassment, fraud, or other harmful conduct.

Do not submit information that you are not authorized to share. Do not rely on CatAI as a substitute for qualified professional advice where such advice is needed.

## 8. Reporting and review

CatAI should provide a clear reporting route when product infrastructure is available. Reports should describe the relevant use, the concern, and any supporting context without unnecessarily exposing personal or confidential information.

Any future enforcement process should be proportionate, transparent, and subject to human review. CatAI should not claim to enforce a rule until the necessary product, contractual, and operational mechanisms exist.
